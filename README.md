# ChurnSight: Customer Retention & Revenue Intelligence Dashboard

A single-file Python project (data cleaning + machine learning + interactive dashboard) that
converts raw subscription-customer data into **business decisions**:

> Data → Information → Insight → Decision → Action

## 1. Problem Statement

A telecom / broadband provider is adding customers every month but is losing a growing share of
them. Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) has started to flatten because churn is catching up with new
sign-ups. Leadership needs to know **what is happening, why customers leave, who is likely to leave
next, and what to do about it**, without reading raw spreadsheets.

## 3. What the Project Does

| Page | Question answered |
|---|---|
| **Executive Overview** | What is happening? 6 KPIs (Active Customers, MRR, Churn Rate, ARPU, Satisfaction, Revenue at Risk), MRR trend, sign-ups vs churn |
| **Revenue & Plan Analysis** | Which plans, services and regions drive revenue? |
| **Customer Risk & Drivers** | Why do customers leave? Who is next? (ML model + priority list) |
| **Risk \| Opportunity \| Action** | What should management do? Auto-generated insights + a retention-campaign ROI simulator |

## 4. Technical Approach

1. **Cleaning**: remove duplicate customers, parse dates, impute missing numbers (median) and categories (mode), clip satisfaction to 1–5.
2. **Feature engineering**: tenure, lifetime revenue, ticket bands, satisfaction bands.
3. **Model**: Random Forest classifier predicting churn (75/25 stratified split). Tenure is excluded to avoid target leakage.
4. **Business layer**: KPIs, churn drivers, revenue at risk (`monthly charge × 12 × churn probability`), and a what-if simulator.

**Model results (held-out test set):** Accuracy 0.72 · ROC-AUC 0.79 · Precision 0.44 · Recall 0.68
(class-weighted to favour recall, because missing a churner costs more than a wasted offer).

## 7. Key Findings

- **West region** churns at 37.3% versus 23.2% overall (1.6x), with more support tickets per customer.
- **Month-to-month contracts** churn at 34.3% versus 9.1% for 2-year contracts.
- Churn rises steadily with support tickets and falls sharply as satisfaction rises.
- Top model drivers: contract type, satisfaction, support tickets, region.
- ~₹1.76 Cr of annual revenue is at risk; 273 high-value customers are at high risk.
- **Recommended action:** targeted retention offer for high-value, high-risk customers (starting with West), plus 1-year contract incentives for low-risk month-to-month customers.

## 9. Limitations

- Data is synthetic, so findings illustrate the method rather than a real company.
- Precision is moderate; the risk list should be used to prioritise outreach, not as certainty.
- Revenue-at-risk assumes the model's probabilities are well calibrated.
